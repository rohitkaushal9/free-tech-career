# 📱 Android Developer

**Kaam:** Android phones ke liye apps banana. India mein zyadatar log Android phone use karte hain, isliye yeh skill yahan bahut kaam ki hai.

## Raasta (order mein)

| Step | Kya seekhna hai | Free mein kahan se | Is step ka project |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Kotlin:** Android ki main language | [roadmap.sh/kotlin](https://roadmap.sh/kotlin) | Chhote console programs |
| 2 | **Android Basics with Compose:** Google ka apna free course | [Android Basics with Compose (Google)](https://developer.android.com/courses/android-basics-compose/course) | Course ke saath bane apps |
| 3 | **Git aur GitHub** | [roadmap.sh/git-github](https://roadmap.sh/git-github) | Saare apps GitHub pe |
| 4 | **Data aur internet:** app mein list, database, API | [roadmap.sh/android](https://roadmap.sh/android) | News ya cricket score dikhane wala app |
| 5 | **Poora map** | [roadmap.sh/android](https://roadmap.sh/android) | Ek app jo dost aur ghar wale sach mein use karein |

## Galtiyan jo time barbaad karti hain

- Purane tutorials (XML layouts) se shuru karna. Naye apps Jetpack Compose se bante hain, Google ka course wahi sikhata hai.
- Bahut bada app soch ke kabhi poora na karna. Chhota app poora karo.
- Sirf emulator pe chalana. Apne phone pe chala ke dekho.
