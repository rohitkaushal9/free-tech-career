# Kaunsa role mere liye?

In 3 sawaalon ka jawab do:

**1. Aapko kya zyada pasand hai?**
- Cheezein banana jo screen pe dikhe → **Web Developer** ya **Android Developer**
- Numbers aur "kyun hua?" ka jawab dhoondhna → **Data Analyst**
- AI aur naye tools → **AI Engineer**

**2. Aapke paas kya hai?**
- Sirf laptop aur internet: chaaron mein se koi bhi shuru ho sakta hai
- Excel pehle se aata hai: **Data Analyst** sabse jaldi shuru hoga

**3. Coding kitni pasand hai?**
- Bahut: **Web**, **Android** ya **AI Engineer**
- Thodi: **Data Analyst** (Excel aur SQL se shuru)

Ek role chuno aur 3 mahine usi pe tike raho. Role badalna hai toh pehle ek project poora karo, fir badlo. Pehle role ki seekhi cheezein agle mein bhi kaam aati hain.
