# Kaunsa role mere liye?

In 3 sawaalon ka jawab do:

**1. Aapko kya zyada pasand hai?**
- Websites banana jo browser mein dikhe → **Web Developer**
- Phone apps banana → **Mobile App Developer (React Native)**
- Data se "kyun hua?" ka jawab dhoondhna aur dashboards → **Data Analyst**
- AI aur naye AI tools → **AI Engineer**

**2. Ek skill, do raaste?**
- Web aur Mobile dono JavaScript/React pe chalte hain. Ek seekh liya, toh doosra jaldi aa jaata hai.

**3. Coding kitni pasand hai?**
- Bahut: **Web**, **Mobile** ya **AI Engineer**
- Thodi kam, sawaal aur numbers zyada: **Data Analyst** (SQL se shuru)

Ek role chuno aur 3 mahine usi pe tike raho. Role badalna hai toh pehle ek project poora karo, fir badlo. Pehle role ki seekhi cheezein agle mein bhi kaam aati hain.
