# 🤖 AI Engineer

**Kaam:** AI wale apps banana. Jaise chatbot, documents se jawab dene wala tool, ya kisi business ka kaam AI se tez karna. Zyadatar AI engineer naye AI model khud nahi banate, bane hue models (jaise ChatGPT, Gemini ke peeche wale) ko apps mein jodte hain.

## Raasta (order mein)

| Step | Kya seekhna hai | Free mein kahan se | Is step ka project |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Python:** AI ki sabse common language | [roadmap.sh/python](https://roadmap.sh/python) | Chhota quiz game ya expense tracker |
| 2 | **Git aur GitHub** | [roadmap.sh/git-github](https://roadmap.sh/git-github) | Projects GitHub pe |
| 3 | **Machine Learning ki basics:** model seekhta kaise hai | [ML for Beginners (Microsoft)](https://github.com/microsoft/ML-For-Beginners) | Ghar ki keemat ya marks ka andaaza lagane wala model |
| 4 | **Generative AI:** prompts, AI apps, chatbot | [Generative AI for Beginners (Microsoft)](https://github.com/microsoft/generative-ai-for-beginners) | Apne notes se sawaal-jawab karne wala chatbot |
| 5 | **AI Engineer ka poora map:** APIs, embeddings, RAG, agents | [roadmap.sh/ai-engineer](https://roadmap.sh/ai-engineer) | Kisi chhote business ke liye ek kaam ka AI tool |

## Galtiyan jo time barbaad karti hain

- Pehle din se bhaari maths mein phans jaana. Step 3 aur 4 karte hue jitni zaroorat ho, utni seekho.
- Sirf prompt likhna seekhna. Job ke liye Python mein AI ko apps se jodna aana chahiye.
- Har hafte naya AI tool try karna, par koi project poora na karna.
