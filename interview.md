# 🎯 Interview mein kya poochte hain?

Zyadatar companies mein fresher ke liye **3 round** hote hain. Har company alag hoti hai, par taiyaari yahi kaam aati hai.

| Round | Kya hota hai | Kaise taiyaari karein |
|---|---|---|
| 1. **Online test (OA)** | DSA ke 2-3 coding sawaal, time limit ke saath | Roz 2 sawaal, easy se medium |
| 2. **Technical** | Apna project samjhao + apne track ke sawaal | Apne project ka har faisla yaad rakho |
| 3. **HR / behavioural** | Aapke kisse: team, galti, mushkil waqt | STAR method se 5 kisse taiyaar rakho |

## 1. DSA (Data Structures & Algorithms)

Pehle yeh topics, isi order mein: **array, string, hashmap, two pointers, stack/queue, linked list, binary search, recursion, tree, graph (BFS/DFS)**. Har sawaal mein yeh bhi socho: code kitna tez chalta hai (Big O).

- Kaunse sawaal kis order mein: [Grind 75 (Tech Interview Handbook)](https://www.techinterviewhandbook.org/grind75)
- DSA ka poora map: [roadmap.sh/datastructures-and-algorithms](https://roadmap.sh/datastructures-and-algorithms)
- Coding interview ki taiyaari, step by step: [Coding interview prep](https://www.techinterviewhandbook.org/coding-interview-prep/)

**Niyam:** ratna nahi, pattern samajhna. Ek sawaal 30 minute mein na bane, toh solution padho, kal khud se dobara likho.

## 2. Apna project: yeh sawaal pakka aate hain

1. Yeh project kya problem solve karta hai, kiske liye?
2. Yeh tech (React, Python, React Native...) kyun chuni?
3. Sabse mushkil bug kya tha, aur kaise theek kiya?
4. Agar 1,000 log ek saath use karein toh kya tootega?
5. Agar dobara banao toh kya alag karoge?

Kisi aur ka copy kiya project yahin pakda jaata hai. Apne project ka har faisla khud samjhao.

## 4. HR round: STAR method

Har kissa 4 hisson mein bolo:
- **S**ituation: kya halaat the
- **T**ask: aapka kaam kya tha
- **A**ction: aapne kya kiya (yahi sabse lamba hissa)
- **R**esult: kya nateeja nikla, number ho toh aur achha

5 kisse pehle se taiyaar rakho: team mein jhagda, apni galti, deadline ka pressure, kuch naya seekha, kisi ki madad ki.
Aur sawaal: [Behavioural interview questions](https://www.techinterviewhandbook.org/behavioral-interview-questions/)

## 5. 30 din ka plan

| Hafta | Roz |
|---|---|
| 1 | 2 easy DSA sawaal + resume theek karo ([Resume guide](https://www.techinterviewhandbook.org/resume/)) |
| 2 | 2 sawaal (easy/medium) + apne project ke 5 sawaalon ke jawab likho |
| 3 | 2 medium sawaal + apne track ke 10 sawaal |
| 4 | Ek dost ke saath mock interview (bol ke jawab do) + 5 STAR kisse |
